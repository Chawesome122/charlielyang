Share Social Media Facebook Twitter Linkedin Digg Reddit Pinterest Email Breadcrumb data Data Releases Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 Data Release September 18, 2026 View Data Release This database contains the United States salient statistics and world production statistics for over 90 nonfuel mineral commodities. Salient statistics for the United States includes items such as production, imports, exports, price, stocks, apparent consumption, and/or net import reliance for nonfuel mineral commodities. The database contains separate table files (entities) in CSV format. There are metadata files associated with these CSV files that describe the data.
