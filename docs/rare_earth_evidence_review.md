# Independent rare-earth evidence review

This is a second-pass source-to-claim review of the archived corporate filings and reports, independent of the root parser. It verifies what a company publicly reported, not the engineering accuracy of a company's assertions. Company discussions of export law are not authoritative legal findings. The source hashes and retrieval timestamps are in `metadata/rare_earth_acquisitions.jsonl`.

## Public-information clocks

SEC filing dates were checked directly in each `RE-*-SUBMISSIONS` archive, matching `primaryDocument` to the archived 10-K. MP's 2025, 2024, 2023 and 2022 reports were filed **2026-02-26, 2025-02-28, 2024-02-28 and 2023-02-28**, respectively. Energy Fuels' corresponding 2025, 2024 and 2023 reports were filed **2026-02-26, 2025-02-26 and 2024-02-23**. Rare Element's were filed **2026-03-12, 2025-03-21 and 2024-03-20**. USA Rare Earth's were filed **2026-03-30, 2025-03-31 and 2024-04-02**. Fiscal period-end dates are not publication dates.

These filing dates are defensible conservative availability dates for claims found in the filings; they do not prove the claim was first publicly known then. References inside a filing to earlier dated announcements provide discovery candidates. Until those originals are archived and adjudicated, preserve `first_public_date_unknown` and a conservative availability upper bound instead of manufacturing a first-public date. A later filing's retrospective history must not leak into an earlier forecast.

Iluka's annual 2025 report is signed **2026-02-18**; its half-year 2026 report is signed **2026-08-19**. These are internal document dates, not independently established exchange-release timestamps. The offtake announcement itself is dated **2026-06-23**. Pensana's construction announcement is dated **2026-01-14**, and its interim release **2026-03-27**. Pensana annual 2025 is signed **2025-10-14**; annual 2024 contains signatures dated **2024-10-29 and 2024-10-30**; annual 2023 contains **2023-10-30**. Archive original exchange publication times before historical scoring.

## Entity continuity and selection

**Visual source check completed during execution:** the original Pensana2024 PDF page5 (spread printed pages6–7) was rendered and read directly. The centre-column paragraph explicitly assigns “continued to be put on hold” to Saltend on-site activity and significant third-party engagements. That source-to-project ambiguity is resolved. The missing independently verified public-release date remains unresolved and excluded from historical estimation.

USA Rare Earth's2023/2024 listed-company reports precede the March 13, 2025 business combination. Its2025 filing explicitly says it was formerly Inflection Point Acquisition Corp.II and describes domesticationMarch 12, mergerMarch 13, Nasdaq tradingMarch 14. Do not treat the pre-combination SPAC's zero industrial revenues or lack of production as USA Rare Earth operating observations, or merge different legal entities without an entity-resolution record. The original operating company's private history requires separate sources.

This source set is purposive discovery evidence, weighted toward issuers with accessible English filings. It is not a statistically complete non-Chinese project universe. It usefully contains suspended/delayed/demo/predevelopment cases but cannot identify population-wide completion hazards. The observed risk set must include independently discovered failures and projects outside these issuer groups before fitting hazards or forecasting probability of China-free service.

## Data admissibility conclusion

Verified claims above establish reported industrial heterogeneity, revisions and physical distinctions. They do not establish a finished empirical LP: magnet recipes, separated Nd/Pr and Dy/Tb allocations, recovery, qualified final demand, transport/capacity/contract constraints and historical stock inventories remain incomplete. No network-independent aggregate capacity total is computed here. Company national-demand equivalences and geopolitical claims are not imported as physical constraints or causal evidence.
