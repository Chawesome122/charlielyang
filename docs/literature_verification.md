# Literature and citation verification

This audit distinguishes a correct bibliographic identity, a publisher-deposited abstract, reading an original document for a specific claim, and independent replication. None implies the next. The blueprint's P/D/M classifications were treated as discovery information and independently checked.

## Evidence acquired

The first 34 attempted requests failed with proxy CONNECT 403 errors. The exact failures remain in `metadata/literature_sources_initial_blocked.csv`. After the environment's network configuration changed and a fresh request succeeded, a justified retry acquired all 24 initial DOI registrations and the original NBER landing page/PDF. Three further scholarly sources lacking DOI identifiers in the blueprint were identified through archived Crossref bibliographic searches and exact registrations. A second identical-book registration for R11 was acquired to investigate a name conflict.

The repository therefore contains **28 DOI registration artifacts covering 27 scholarly source identities**, three discovery-search responses, the NBER R02 landing page and original PDF. Registration metadata are in `data/raw/literature/*_crossref.json`; download dates, resolved URLs, SHA256 hashes and failures are recorded in `metadata/literature_sources*.csv`. The NBER PDF cover explicitly dates its revision to April 2025. All 28 registration deposit timestamps predate the 2026-10-11 freeze. Current indexing/retrieval dates are bookkeeping and do not become historical policy or economic observations.

Full publisher article attempts for R01, R03, R04, R05, R06, R07 and R36 remain inaccessible through the attempted routes (403/tunnel denial). These failures do not establish missing credentials or permission to bypass publisher restrictions. Available publisher-deposited abstracts establish their limited exact contents; full methods, sample construction, paywall data caveats and appendix assumptions remain unverified.

`scripts/fetch_literature.py` repeats explicit acquisition without disabling TLS or signature checks. Reruns version changed source bytes and retain the preceding request manifest. `scripts/build_citation_audit.py` rebuilds `manuscript/references.bib` and `metadata/citation_audit.csv` entirely offline. The latter has one row per blueprint source R01–R40 and records the exact level of reading, first publication, registration deposit date, claim, excerpt, evidence path and limitations. Agency entries are linked to their separately archived component evidence when reviewed.

## Errors, sparse metadata and unresolved checks

- R08's DOI record verifies title, journal, year, volume and pages but omits the author. “Bloom, N.” in the working BibTeX remains explicitly provisional from the blueprint until an original publisher/author document is read.
- R11's Princeton DOI10.1515/9781400830176 lists “Robert K.Dixit”; its identical-book relation to DOI10.2307/j.ctt7sncv lists “Avinash K.Dixit.” Both have been archived. The conventional Avinash K.Dixit entry is retained with an explicit conflict note; the disagreement is not silently erased.
- R09 registration provides an online2015 publication date and articleidentifierhhv050 but no final volume/issue/pages. The working BibTeX follows those verified sparse fields; the blueprint's2016 issue must be checked against the original before final journal formatting.
- R19's deposited title omits the blueprint's “I.Observable actions” subtitle; R20 uses a modern Hiroshima Mathematical Journal container rather than an independently checked original1964 Series A-I scan. Neither difference is silently declared an error in the blueprint.
- R29's official Japanese index/full legal title remains inaccessible in the component acquisition. R31's blueprint URL had an appended `%3A11` artifact; a corrected URL is a discovery candidate, not legal evidence until read.
- Every undated live data/manual entry needs version/publication awareness before it can support a historical estimate. A bibliography citation is not a downloaded database or a rights clearance.
