# Documented mathematical and numerical modifications

The blueprint remains the methodological authority. The following repairs preserve its research architecture and are supported by executed tests.

1. **Reduced resilience ODE at zero rates (§5.1).** Allowed `I=delta=0` gives `A+delta=0`. Every state is then a neutral equilibrium, so the quotient formula and strict negative eigenvalue require `A+delta>0`. See `dynamics/resilience.py`, `tests/test_dynamics.py` and `outputs/synthetic/physical/ode_diagnostics.json`.
2. **Logistic critical boundary (§5.2).** At beta=4, theta=-2 the only root is 1/2 with a zero linear eigenvalue and stabilizing cubic drift. It is neither an ordinary fold nor evidence of industrial hysteresis. Beta>4 is needed for a three-root interval.
3. **Singular action game (§8.4).** Delta=0 may produce a compatible line of equilibria. The active-set solver records continuum information and box intersections rather than declaring that no interior equilibrium exists solely because inversion fails. Tests check singular and ordinary regimes independently.
4. **Discrete HJB policy improvement (§8.6).** Own action changes the sign of a pipeline drift. A frozen forward/backward derivative can therefore optimize the wrong discrete Hamiltonian. The implementation retains the exact nine continuous-gradient regimes and adds a five-status-per-player discrete enumeration (zero, lower-piece interior, kink, upper-piece interior, one), checking global unilateral deviations. This is a numerical discretization correction, not a different continuous model.
5. **Interpolated feedback stability (§8.6).** A central secant at a grid knot can falsely suggest stability of a nonsmooth policy. Such stationary points now receive `locally_stable=null` and an explicit knot warning. Off-knot diagnostics include policy derivatives. The independent adversarial test constructs an unstable one-sided drift that a central secant misses.
6. **Sparse linear recovery.** BiCGSTAB stagnated while evaluating a 17-node policy on the 21-node grid. The discounted operator is still a nonsingular M-matrix. A GMRES fallback resolves that linear solve with unchanged residual thresholds; solver codes, methods and residuals are retained. A direct sparse-solve comparison tests the fallback. This correction does not repair the negative-beta nonlinear-game failures.
7. **Iteration budget.** Four positive-beta 21-node starts initially reached the 80-step limit with decreasing action errors about 1.02e-7–1.10e-7, just above the fixed 1e-7 threshold. The positive fine-grid budget was extended to120, without relaxing acceptance. Original attempts are preserved under `outputs/diagnostics/initial_positive_21_iteration_limit/`. Negative-beta failures are separately retained.
8. **Stateless forecast/LP bridge.** A single-quarter callback cannot carry stock across quarters. It rejects networks with opening inventory/storage rather than repeatedly replenishing stock. The underlying multi-quarter LP conserves inventories. The callback also returns an explicitly defined uncapped potential measure instead of an unusable NaN.
9. **Backtest admissibility.** An origin dictionary key does not establish an historical information set. Scoring additionally requires a vintage identifier, prediction-as-of date, and registered publication clocks for inputs; future training releases are rejected before prediction callbacks run. This is a source-admissibility contract, not a claim of complete historical project data.

No empirical coefficient, technological capability score, engineering recipe or national utility was introduced to make an unresolved requirement appear complete.
# Additional finite-batch accounting diagnostic

The later industrial extraction adds a diagnostic, without changing the blueprint's process LP or game. Conditional on reported batch masses, let $M_o$ denote oxide input, $M_m$ finished-magnet output, $M_a$ all other inputs (including inventory draw and process materials), and $M_w\ge0$ all other outputs. Conservation requires $M_o+M_a=M_m+M_w$, hence $M_a\ge\max(0,M_m-M_o)$. For the issuer's approximate 1.2/3.0-tonne quantities, this is approximately 1.8 tonnes. It is a necessary total-mass inequality only: no elemental composition, chemical recovery, input origin, production interval or future input availability is identified. The 2.5 native-product ratio is not a recovery fraction. Tests verify this distinction and reject nonfinite/nonpositive batch values.
