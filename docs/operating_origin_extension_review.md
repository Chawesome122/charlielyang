# MP operating routing, ownership and origin evidence

Information freeze: **2026-10-11**. This extension adds 33 previously unextracted claims from three archived MP Materials Forms 10-K, including 14 numerical values. Original-byte SHA256, exact normalized quotations, source match counts/ordinals, character locators, issuer filing clocks, retrieval timestamps, native units and event-date intervals are retained. The conservative selected original public dates are 2024-02-28, 2025-02-28 and 2026-02-26. They do not certify globally earliest disclosure, release-time source bytes or independently observed operating truth.

## Source findings

The FY2023 and FY2024 reports disclose MP's **49%** and Shenghe's **51%** equity interests in VREX Holdco, incorporated in Singapore, which wholly owns the Vietnam operating metal-processing company. The filings state that Shenghe manages day-to-day operations and that the board is not controlled by MP. Shenghe Singapore is described as an indirect majority-owned subsidiary of Shanghai-listed Shenghe Resources Holding. These are direct ownership/management/affiliation disclosures, not a complete legal-control determination inferred solely from a stock-exchange listing.

The FY2023 filing reports use of a Southeast Asian tolling partner beginning in Q4 2023. The FY2024 filing explicitly identifies oxide delivered to VREX Holdco and converted to metal by VREX, with MP retaining title to products and entering customer sales agreements. Product ownership, physical processing location and ownership/management of processing assets are therefore distinct. The statements do not supply lot-level mine origin, mass throughput, conversion recoveries, auxiliary-input origins or qualified final-magnet service.

The FY2025 filing reports MP sold its 49% VREX interest in **May 2025**, receiving USD 9.7 million. The old 49/51 snapshot cannot be carried forward as current ownership. The sale does not identify remaining owners' percentages, a factory closure, a cancellation or the end of tolling. No such transition is invented. Exact disposal day is unknown; May 1–31 is an outer interval. The report also states that sales of MP products to China ceased in **July 2025**, and that its Materials segment began supplying Mountain Pass NdPr oxide internally to the Independence Magnetics operation in **Q4 2025**. Outbound sales cessation and domestic internal feed do not certify all inputs' origins or a completely China-free magnet route. Manufacturing/commissioning at the end of 2025 is not stable independently qualified commercial throughput.

The filings preserve purchases of flotation reagents and other materials via Shenghe. There are nine comparative-vintage numerical observations over five reported years. Selected disclosed nominal amounts (USD million) are 4.8 in 2021, 18.5 in 2022, 8.3 in 2023, 4.8 in 2024 and 19.7 in 2025. **The FY2025 source qualifies its amounts to periods when Shenghe was a related party, and says that status ended starting in Q4 2025.** The 19.7 amount is not silently annualized, and no share of total inputs is identified. A procurement counterparty is not proof of where the reagents were manufactured. Dollars are not physical feed, a chemical recovery or a causal adaptation outcome. Across overlapping source vintages, the disclosed numerical amounts agree; that agreement is not independent replication or proof that all purchases are covered.
