# Korean Metals Plant production, dispatch and route evidence

Five issuer quarterly reports cover 2025 Q2 through 2026 Q2. The pipeline verifies each report's exchange dateline on page 1, reporting period, SHA256 and explicit claim page. The original archived snapshots were retrieved after the research freeze. Each source's public-by date governs historical selection. Eleven information origins replay 34 selected product and period records, including both sides of release dates.

## Measured disclosures and comparisons

- Reported NdFeB strip-alloy dispatches were 13.9 tonnes in 2025 Q3, approximately 24.3 in Q4, 42 in 2026 Q1 and 36 in Q2. Later quarterly comparatives retain distinct claim IDs and their own clocks. The quarter with selected 2025 Q2 customer deliveries has no textual plant-wide alloy total in this extraction.
- The July 30 report gives approximately 36 tonnes of alloy and nine tonnes of the combined Nd and NdPr metal category in Q2, compared with about 42 and 0.6 tonnes in Q1. Alloy falls six tonnes and mixed metal rises 8.4 tonnes in the printed figures. The Q2 component sum is 45 tonnes. The component-based aggregate rise is 2.4 tonnes from rounded figures, alongside the issuer prose's approximately 2.5 tonnes. These are dispatches, with separate source language about production increasing.
- Q3 2025 reports approximately 16.7 tonnes of alloy produced and 13.9 dispatched, and 1.7 tonnes of NdPr metal produced and 22.2 dispatched. The corresponding production-minus-dispatch differences are +2.8 and −20.5 tonnes. Unobserved starting inventory, third-party receipts, internal transfers and losses prevent a closed stock ledger. The two products have different compositions.
- An unnamed customer's 84-tonne alloy order was disclosed after 2026 Q1. The next report gives a reduced 50 tonnes for calendar 2026. Both quantities describe prospective supply. The subsequent report confirms commencement of processing and dispatch under the previously announced customer-feed tolling agreement. The named customers in the Q2 alloy account have no individual tonnage allocations in that account.

## Finite route episodes

- The June 2025 Noveon shipment is 1.2 tonnes of alloy, with a separate schedule for the remainder of the 15-tonne allocation under its wider agreement. VAC dispatch of 1.2 tonnes on June 30 and receipt in the first week of July describe one shipment. A 100 kg USA Rare Earth dispatch and a 100 kg GKN sample receipt retain separate roles. The reported completion of a 19-tonne Magnequench NdPr-metal order provides no period allocation for the full order.
- The 2025 Q3 report identifies a shipment of 2 kg Tb metal and 2 kg Dy metal to the Narva facility through Magnequench. The Q4 report states that a further 13 kg Dy was produced and delivered to Neo Performance Materials in December. The record retains the more specific destination for the initial shipment only. The Q1 disclosure separately reports 10 kg of Tb produced.
- In 2025 Q2 ASM reported receiving two tonnes of unspecified oxides from an Australian supplier, alongside existing stock usage. In 2026 Q1 it reported a shipment of NdPr oxide from a non-China source without a quantity. Neither source links an input lot and its complete origin to one of the named downstream shipments.

The seven documented episodes provide finite producer-reported flows. Qualified grade and element composition, lot genealogy, buyer usage, auxiliary origins, dated stocks, process recovery, steady route capacity, representative surveillance and matched final demand remain unresolved. No newly identified physical network input is used as a complete service coefficient. The October SEC 8-K was excluded on its release date.
